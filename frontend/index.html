Balances aren't stored.
They're derived.

This client talks to a ledger service where every transfer writes an immutable debit and credit entry. An account's balance is never a mutable field — it's computed on demand from the entries that produced it.

  • Every transfer is atomic — it fully commits or fully rolls back.
  • Idempotency keys mean a retried request never double-charges.
  • Ledger entries are append-only and cannot be edited or deleted.
